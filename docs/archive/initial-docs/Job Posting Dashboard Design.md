## **Job Posting Dashboard Design**

Here's a detailed design outline for your client-facing Job Posting Dashboard, incorporating AI, analytics, and a user-friendly experience.

### **Overall Goals**

* **Empower Clients:** Provide tools to create effective job postings and track their performance.  
* **Streamline Workflow:** Simplify the job posting process with AI assistance and templates.  
* **Enhance Engagement:** Offer a visually appealing and intuitive interface.  
* **Data-Driven Decisions:** Enable clients to optimize their hiring strategies with comprehensive analytics.  
* **Provide Support:** Offer multiple support channels to address client needs.

### **Key Features and Sections**

1. **Sidebar Navigation:**  
   * **Dashboard:** Overview of key metrics and recent activity.  
   * **Post a Job:** Access the AI job post generator and templates.  
   * **Analytics:** View performance data for job postings.  
   * **Templates:** Browse and select job post templates.  
   * **Branding:** Customize the appearance of job postings.  
   * **Support:** Access help resources and contact support.  
2. **Header:**  
   * Company Logo: Display the client's logo.  
   * Notifications: Alert clients of new applications or updates.  
   * Profile: Access account settings and manage profile information.  
3. **Main Content Area:**  
   * **Onboarding Wizard:**  
     * Interactive guide for new users.  
     * Step-by-step instructions for creating a job post, navigating the dashboard, and understanding key features.  
     * Progress tracking to show completion status.  
     * Tooltips and helpful hints.  
   * **AI Job Post Generator:**  
     * Form with fields for job title, description, responsibilities, requirements, and location.  
     * AI-powered suggestions for:  
       * Keywords: Suggest relevant keywords to improve visibility.  
       * Phrasing: Offer alternative wording for clarity and impact.  
       * Formatting: Automatically format the job description for readability.  
     * Live Preview:  
       * Real-time preview of the job posting as the client enters information.  
       * Displays how the posting will appear on different platforms (desktop, mobile).  
     * "Generate First Draft" button:  
       * Uses AI to create a complete job post based on the provided information.  
     * Options to refine tone and detail.  
   * **Templates:**  
     * Library of pre-designed job post templates for various roles and industries.  
     * Templates are auto-populated with sample content.  
     * Clients can easily customize templates to fit their needs.  
     * Thumbnail previews of each template.  
   * **Branding:**  
     * Options to customize the appearance of job postings:  
       * Logo Upload: Upload the company logo.  
       * Color Palette: Select a color scheme that aligns with the company brand.  
       * Font Selection: Choose from a variety of fonts.  
     * Live Preview: Changes to branding are reflected in the live preview.  
   * **Analytics:**  
     * Dashboard with key metrics:  
       * Impressions: Number of times the job posting has been viewed.  
       * Clicks: Number of times the job posting has been clicked.  
       * Click-Through Rate (CTR): Percentage of impressions that resulted in clicks.  
       * Applications: Number of applications received.  
       * Apply-Start Rate  
     * Demographic data of applicants:  
       * Gender: Breakdown of applicants by gender (e.g., male/female ratio).  
       * Age: Age distribution of applicants (e.g., age ranges).  
       * Location: Geographic distribution of applicants (e.g., city, region).  
     * Funnel Effectiveness:  
       * Applicants per opening  
       * Application completion rate  
       * Quality-of-hire trends  
     * Customizable date ranges and filters.  
     * Data visualization:  
       * Charts (bar, line, pie) to display metrics.  
       * Heatmaps to visualize applicant locations.  
     * Smart Narrative Summaries  
   * **Support:**  
     * Embedded Chat:  
       * Persistent chat widget for instant support.  
       * AI-powered chatbot for initial inquiries.  
       * Option to connect with a live support agent.  
       * Context-aware prompts.  
     * Resource Center:  
       * Links to FAQs, video tutorials, and best-practice articles.

### **Design Principles**

* **Minimalist Design:**  
  * Clean layout with ample whitespace.  
  * Focus on essential information and actions.  
* **Intuitive Navigation:**  
  * Clear and consistent navigation.  
  * Easy-to-find features.  
* **User-Friendly Interface:**  
  * Simple and straightforward forms.  
  * Clear calls to action (CTAs).  
  * Consistent use of UI elements.  
* **Data Visualization:**  
  * Use charts and graphs to present data in an easily understandable format.  
* **Accessibility:**  
  * Ensure the dashboard is accessible to users with disabilities.  
  * Use color-blind-friendly palettes.  
* **Responsiveness:**  
  * The dashboard should be responsive and work well on different screen sizes (desktop, tablet, mobile).
